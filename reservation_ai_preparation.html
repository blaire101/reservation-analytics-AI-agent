Analytics Safety

The LLM does not write arbitrary SQL. It extracts business context. The application validates that context, resolves one campaign ID, and then executes controlled SQL templates.

30-second Answer

I built a reservation analytics data mart on AWS using S3, Glue, the Glue Data Catalog, and Athena. The data mart combines reservation and order data at the user, campaign, product, and site grain. On top of that data mart, I built a small AI prototype for operations users. I used LlamaIndex for business and metric knowledge retrieval, while actual analytical questions were handled by an Athena analytics tool. LangGraph routed the request, and I validated country, product, and campaign before querying data. If a campaign was ambiguous, the agent asked the user to clarify instead of guessing.

Why LlamaIndex?

It is the business knowledge layer for approved definitions, metrics, metadata, and campaign rules.

Why Athena instead of RAG for numbers?

Counts and conversion rates must come from the actual Data Mart, not vector similarity retrieval.

Why campaign_id?

Campaign names and month descriptions may be ambiguous. The resolver produces one governed analytical key before the DM query runs.

What did you own?

I designed and developed the Data Mart and backend agent service. Another engineer handled deployment and Feishu integration.
